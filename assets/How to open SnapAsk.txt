SnapAsk: drag across a question on your screen, get a one-line AI answer.
Needs a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or newer) and macOS 13 or later.

1. Drag SnapAsk into the Applications folder, then open it from Applications.

2. If macOS says it can't verify SnapAsk (it isn't from the App Store):
   click Done, open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down and
   click "Open Anyway" next to SnapAsk, then confirm. You only do this once.

3. SnapAsk lives in the menu bar (the viewfinder icon), not the Dock.
   It asks for two permissions: Accessibility (for its shortcuts) and
   Screen Recording (to capture what you select). Turn SnapAsk on in both
   lists, then quit and reopen it.

4. Paste your own API key (Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini) in the SnapAsk window
   and click Save. It stays in your Mac's Keychain, nowhere else.

5. Press Control-A, then drag over a question. The answer appears in the
   bottom-left corner; click it to copy. Escape cancels.
